# Privacy Policy for DormDesk

**Last updated:** November 20, 2026

DormDesk ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting the privacy and personal information of hostel residents, students, and administration staff ("users", "you"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, disclosed, and safeguarded when you use the DormDesk mobile application ("the App").

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Personal Information
- **Account Data:** When registering or using an account, we may collect your name, email address, student ID, room/block number, and hostel designation (Student or Warden).
- **Authentication Credentials:** Encrypted passwords and session authentication tokens.

### B. User-Generated Content & Operational Data
- **Maintenance & Support Requests:** Information submitted when raising complaints, tickets, or service requests (issue category, room location, description, urgency/priority tag).
- **Photos & Media:** Optional photo attachments uploaded to illustrate maintenance issues or facility repair requirements.
- **Feedback & Suggestions:** Categorized suggestions regarding hostel amenities, dining/mess, cleanliness, or administration (including submissions designated as anonymous).
- **Ratings & Verification:** Satisfaction ratings (1 to 5 stars) and resolution confirmation notes.

### C. Technical & App Diagnostics
- Standard non-identifying diagnostics required to maintain app performance, prevent crashes, and ensure system reliability.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We process and utilize your information exclusively for legitimate hostel administration and app functionality:
1. Facilitating the assignment, tracking, and resolution of maintenance complaints.
2. Broadcasting official hostel notices, circulars, and emergency announcements.
3. Enabling resident-to-administration communication and resolution verification.
4. Providing 24/7 in-app automated FAQ assistance (DormDesk AI).
5. Generating aggregated, non-identifying satisfaction and maintenance statistics for hostel administration.

---

## 3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosure

- **Zero Third-Party Advertising:** DormDesk does not contain third-party advertisements or trackers.
- **Zero Commercial Data Selling:** We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize personal user data under any circumstances.
- **Internal Administrative Sharing Only:** Maintenance tickets and room numbers are shared strictly with authorized hostel wardens and assigned maintenance technicians solely to fulfill repair duties.

---

## 4. Data Security & Storage

- All data transmitted between the App and backend servers is protected using industry-standard **HTTPS / TLS encryption in transit**.
- Authentication credentials and session tokens are encrypted and securely stored.

---

## 5. Account Deletion & User Rights

In compliance with Google Play Store policies and privacy regulations:
- **Right to Access & Rectify:** Users can review and edit their profile information directly within the App settings.
- **Account & Data Deletion:** Users may request the permanent deletion of their account, complaint history, and personal data at any time by contacting administration or via **Profile > Settings > Delete Account / Request Deletion**, or by emailing support at `privacy@dormdesk.app`.
- **Immediate Data Purging:** Upon account deletion request, personal user identifiers and uploaded photos are permanently purged from active systems.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

DormDesk is designed for college and university hostel residents and adult staff members. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 1, 2026

DormDesk ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your personal information when you use the DormDesk mobile application (the "App"). By using DormDesk, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information to provide and improve our hostel management services:

1.1 Account Information

  • Full name – used to identify you within the hostel system.
  • Email address – used for account authentication and communication.
  • Phone number – used for contact purposes related to complaint resolution.
  • Student ID – used to verify your enrollment and hostel residency.
  • Hostel details – including hostel name, room number, and block, used to route complaints and services to the appropriate administrators.

1.2 Complaint Data

  • Complaint descriptions – text you provide when filing a complaint.
  • Photographs – images you attach to complaints to document issues.
  • Complaint status and history – records of complaint progress and resolution.

1.3 Feedback Data

  • Feedback submissions – text and ratings you provide about hostel services and facilities.

1.4 Chat Messages

  • AI chatbot conversations – messages you send to and receive from the DormDesk AI assistant.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used exclusively for the following purposes:

  • Hostel management: To facilitate communication between students and hostel administrators, assign rooms, and manage hostel operations.
  • Complaint tracking: To register, route, track, and resolve complaints you submit about hostel facilities and services.
  • AI chatbot assistance: To provide automated responses to common hostel-related queries through our AI-powered chatbot.
  • Service improvement: To analyze aggregated, anonymized usage patterns and improve the App's features and user experience.
  • Communication: To send you updates regarding your complaints, important hostel announcements, and policy changes.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using the following services:

  • Firebase Authentication: Manages your account credentials, login sessions, and identity verification. Your password is never stored in plain text.
  • Firebase Realtime Database: Stores your profile information, complaints, feedback, and chat history in a secure, cloud-hosted database.
  • Firebase Storage: Stores photographs and media files you upload with your complaints.

All Firebase services are provided by Google and comply with industry-standard security practices. Data is stored on servers located in secure data centers with encryption at rest and in transit.

4. Third-Party Services

DormDesk integrates with the following third-party services:

4.1 Groq AI

Our AI chatbot feature is powered by Groq. When you use the chatbot, only your chat query text is sent to Groq's API for processing. We do not send any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email, student ID, or hostel details to Groq. Groq processes the query and returns a response, which is then displayed in the App.

4.2 Firebase (Google)

We use Firebase services for authentication, database, and file storage as described in Section 3. Firebase's privacy practices are governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Data Sharing Policy

We take your privacy seriously:

  • We do not sell your personal data to any third party.
  • We do not share your personal information with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.
  • Your data is shared only with Groq for the sole purpose of processing AI chatbot queries, and only the query text is shared — no PII.
  • We may disclose your information if required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request.
  • Hostel administrators have access to complaint and feedback data to fulfill their management responsibilities.

6. Data Retention

  • Active accounts: Your data is retained for as long as your account remains active and you continue to use the App.
  • Account deletion: When you delete your account, all associated personal data — including your profile, complaints, feedback, and chat history — is permanently deleted from our systems.
  • Anonymized data: Aggregated, anonymized complaint statistics may be retained for hostel improvement purposes, but these cannot be traced back to any individual user.

7. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: You can view all your personal information, complaints, feedback, and chat history within the App at any time.
  • Correction: You can update and correct your personal information through the Profile screen in the App.
  • Deletion: You can request deletion of your personal data by deleting your account from the Profile screen, or by contacting us at support@dormdesk.app.
  • Data portability: You can contact us to request a copy of your personal data in a standard format.

8. Account Deletion

DormDesk provides a straightforward account deletion process. You can delete your account directly from the Profile screen within the App. Upon deletion:

  • All personal information (name, email, phone, student ID, hostel details) is permanently removed.
  • All complaints you have filed, including attached photographs, are permanently deleted.
  • All feedback submissions are permanently deleted.
  • All AI chatbot conversation history is permanently deleted.
  • Your Firebase Authentication account is permanently removed.

This action is irreversible. Once your account is deleted, your data cannot be recovered. For more details, please refer to our Data Deletion Policy.

9. Children's Privacy

DormDesk is designed for college and university students and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected data from a child under 13, we will take immediate steps to delete that information from our systems. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us at support@dormdesk.app.

10. Security Measures

We implement the following security measures to protect your data:

  • HTTPS encryption: All data transmitted between the App and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS protocols.
  • Firebase Security Rules: Strict database security rules ensure that users can only access their own data, and administrators can only access data within their jurisdiction.
  • API key protection: Sensitive API keys (such as the Groq AI key) are never stored client-side. They are managed securely through server-side configurations.
  • Authentication: Firebase Authentication provides secure sign-in with password hashing, session management, and brute-force protection.
  • Access control: Role-based access control ensures that students, wardens, and administrators can only access data appropriate to their roles.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, or other factors. When we make significant changes:

  • Users will be notified via in-app announcements.
  • The "Effective Date" at the top of this policy will be updated.
  • Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your data.

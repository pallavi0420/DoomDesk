Data Deletion Policy

Effective Date: November 1, 2026

At DormDesk, we respect your right to control your personal data. This Data Deletion Policy explains how you can request the deletion of your data, what data is deleted, what data may be retained, and the timeline for processing deletion requests.

1. How to Request Data Deletion

1.1 In-App Deletion (Recommended)

The fastest and easiest way to delete your data is through the App itself:

  1. Open the DormDesk app on your device.
  2. Navigate to the Profile screen.
  3. Tap "Delete Account".
  4. Read the confirmation message carefully. It will inform you that all your data will be permanently removed.
  5. Tap "Delete Permanently" to confirm.
  6. Your account and all associated data will be permanently deleted.

1.2 Manual Deletion Request

If you are unable to access the App or prefer to request deletion manually, you can contact our support team:

Email: support@dormdesk.app

Subject: Data Deletion Request

Include: Your registered email address and student ID for identity verification.

We will verify your identity before processing any manual deletion request to protect against unauthorized requests.

2. What Data Is Deleted

When you delete your account, the following data is permanently removed from all our systems:

Data Category Details
Personal Information Name, email address, phone number, student ID, hostel details (hostel name, room number, block)
Complaints All complaints you have filed, including descriptions, status history, and timestamps
Photographs All images and media files you uploaded as part of complaints
Feedback All feedback submissions, ratings, and comments
Chat History All AI chatbot conversation messages and history
Authentication Data Your Firebase Authentication account, login credentials, and session data

3. What Data May Be Retained

In limited circumstances, certain data may be retained after account deletion:

  • Anonymized complaint statistics: Aggregated, anonymized data derived from complaints (such as the total number of complaints about a particular facility or issue category) may be retained for the purpose of hostel improvement and operational analytics. This data is fully anonymized and cannot be traced back to any individual user.
  • Legal obligations: If we are required by law, regulation, or legal process to retain certain data, we will do so for the minimum period required and only to the extent necessary to comply with such obligations.

No personally identifiable information is retained after account deletion except as required by law.

4. Deletion Timeline

  • In-app deletion: When you delete your account through the App, the deletion process begins immediately. Your data is removed from active systems in real time.
  • Manual deletion requests: Manual requests submitted via email will be processed within 30 days of receiving and verifying your request.
  • Backup systems: Data may persist in encrypted backup systems for up to 30 days after deletion, after which it is permanently purged.

⚠ Important: Account deletion is permanent and irreversible. Once your data is deleted, it cannot be recovered. Please ensure you want to proceed before confirming deletion. If you wish to use DormDesk again after deletion, you will need to create a new account.

5. Confirmation Process

To prevent accidental deletions, DormDesk uses a multi-step confirmation process:

  1. Initiation: You tap "Delete Account" on the Profile screen.
  2. Confirmation dialog: A clear confirmation dialog is displayed, explaining exactly what will be deleted and that the action is irreversible.
  3. Final confirmation: You must tap "Delete Permanently" to proceed. Tapping "Cancel" will abort the process and no data will be affected.
  4. Completion: Upon confirmation, your data is permanently removed, your session is ended, and you are returned to the login screen.

For manual deletion requests via email, we will send a confirmation email to your registered address to verify the request before processing it.

6. Contact for Deletion Requests

If you have any questions about data deletion, need assistance, or wish to submit a manual deletion request, please contact us:

DormDesk Support Team

Email: support@dormdesk.app

Subject Line: Data Deletion Request

We are committed to processing all deletion requests promptly and in compliance with applicable data protection regulations.
